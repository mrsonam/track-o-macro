## User-facing copy

When writing or editing **product copy** (UI strings, errors, onboarding, marketing, help text), follow `PRODUCT.md` → **Copy and punctuation**:

- **No em dashes, en dashes, or `--`** to join phrases or clauses.
- Use commas, colons, periods, or parentheses instead.
- For ranges, use **to** (for example `13 to 120`), not an en dash.

Compound-word hyphens in dictionary forms (for example `USDA-backed`) are fine.
